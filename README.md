# Awesome Personal CRM [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of apps and resources for remembering people, recording context, and following up with care.

Personal CRM tools help individuals keep useful relationship context without treating every person like a sales opportunity. This list includes proprietary services and open-source projects, with clear labels so readers can compare them on equal terms.

## Apps

- [Cloze](https://www.cloze.com/) - **Proprietary.** Relationship management platform that combines contact history, communication context, follow-ups, and professional workflows.
- [Covve](https://covve.com/personal-crm) - **Proprietary.** Personal CRM and contact manager with notes, reminders, relationship prompts, and contact enrichment.
- [Dex](https://getdex.com/) - **Proprietary.** Personal and professional relationship manager with contact notes, reminders, and integrations.
- [Ember](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6770759791) - **Proprietary.** Mobile personal CRM for saving relationship context, setting reminders, and consulting an AI Keeper about information the user has recorded.
- [folk](https://www.folk.app/) - **Proprietary.** Collaborative CRM for organizing contacts, enrichment, messaging, pipelines, and relationship workflows.
- [harperreed/crm](https://github.com/harperreed/crm) - **Open source.** Personal CRM with web and terminal interfaces plus contact sync and automation integrations.
- [HiHello](https://www.hihello.com/features/business-contact-manager) - **Proprietary.** Digital business card and contact management product with notes, tags, scanning, and team address book features.
- [looot-network](https://github.com/loootai/looot-network) - **Open source.** Self-hostable app that imports LinkedIn, Google Contacts, X or CSV exports into the user's own Supabase project and searches those people in plain language, with optional paid enrichment.
- [Mesh](https://me.sh/) - **Proprietary.** Relationship and contact network, formerly Clay, that consolidates people data, updates, notes, and reminders.
- [Monica](https://www.monicahq.com/) - **Open source.** Personal relationship manager for recording people, interactions, reminders, and life details, available as hosted software or for self-hosting.
- [peopleOS](https://github.com/djaiss/peopleOS) - **Open source.** Self-hostable personal CRM for keeping structured information about people and relationships.
- [PingCRM](https://github.com/sneg55/pingcrm) - **Open source.** Self-hostable personal networking CRM with contact organization and relationship management features.
- [UpHabit](https://uphabit.com/) - **Proprietary.** Personal CRM for maintaining business relationships through contact aggregation, notes, tags, and follow-up reminders.

## Methods & Systems

- [Getting Things Done](https://gettingthingsdone.com/what-is-gtd/) - Capture and review system that can be adapted to relationship follow-ups and other next actions.
- [How to Build a Simple Personal CRM System](https://dextr.app/articles/how-to-build-a-simple-personal-crm-system/) - Vendor-authored, step-by-step framework for setting up and maintaining a personal CRM.
- [How to Use a Personal CRM for Better Networking](https://getdex.com/blog/personal-crm-networking-guide/) - Vendor-authored seven-step workflow for importing, organizing, reviewing, and following up with contacts.
- [PARA Method](https://fortelabs.com/blog/para/) - General information organization system for projects, areas, resources, and archives that can also structure relationship notes.

## Data Portability

- [Apple iCloud: Import, export, or print contacts](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/icloud/mmfba748b2/icloud) - Official instructions for moving contacts with vCard files.
- [European Commission: Data protection under GDPR](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/legal-framework-eu-data-protection_en) - Primary-source overview of European data protection rights, including data portability.
- [Google Contacts: Export, back up, or restore contacts](https://support.google.com/contacts/answer/7199294) - Official instructions for exporting contacts as Google CSV, Outlook CSV, or vCard.
- [RFC 6350: vCard Format Specification](https://www.rfc-editor.org/info/rfc6350/) - Open standard for exchanging contact information between systems.
